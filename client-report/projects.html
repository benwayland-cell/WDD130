General Experience and Reflections

What was the most challenging part of this project for you? Were you able to overcome it?

The most challenging part of this project was organization. It is extremely confusing to be handling four different projects at once. There were also communication problems where some people just wouldn't do any of the work that they were assigned. I think I was able enough to get a finished product for what I was assigned.

What did you enjoy most about this project?

It was interesting to figure out how to do things in various different roles.

What would you do differently if you could do the project again?

I would more forcefully ask those above and below me for information on what they are doing.

Do you feel more confident in working on real-world projects after this experience? Explain.

I think I do feel a bit more confident. The thing I have learned most is how to deal with people who don't respond. I also have gotten a bit of a feel for how to organize tasks between people.
